## Findings requiring action

1. **KvB87: correct the author order.** The original article and DOI metadata both put **Pierre van Baal first and Jeffrey Koller second**. Use `P.~van Baal and J.~Koller`, retaining the existing title, journal, year, volume, pages and DOI. The original first page is preserved in [van Baal's institutional article archive](https://www.ilorentz.org/research/vanbaal/DECEASED/HOME/JOUR/APNY174_299.pdf).
2. **MJ00: distinguish the cited print edition from a conflicting publisher metadata date.** The manuscript gives 2000. The [coauthor's official St Andrews publication record](https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/directional-statistics/) gives 2000 for print ISBN 0-471-95333-4. The [Wiley DOI landing page](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9780470316979) and Crossref label first publication as 3 January 1999, while online publication is 2008. This is a real cataloguing discrepancy; the DOI resolves to the right title and authors. Retaining **2000 as the print-edition year** is supported by the coauthor's institution. Do not silently change the year to 1999 or label the citation fabricated.
3. **vRS05: optional exact-title punctuation.** The publisher title is “Transport inequalities, gradient estimates, entropy and Ricci curvature”, without a comma after “entropy”. The manuscript's extra comma is stylistic, not a different publication. Its 2005 print year is correct; first online publication was 2004.
