## Corrections and edition details

- **Bar37.** The two original pages confirm the title, the byline Joseph Barta, volume 204, issue 7, and pages 472–473. Page 473 identifies the session of 15 February 1937 and contains inequality (5). The Gallica endpoint returned HTTP 403 during this audit. The same scan was successfully inspected through the Wikimedia facsimile linked below.
- **KvB87.** The correct author order is Pierre van Baal and Jeffrey Koller. The original article and publisher-deposited DOI record agree. The manuscript now gives this order.
- **KS75.** The original Kogut–Susskind Hamiltonian paper is by John Kogut and Leonard Susskind, *Physical Review D* **11**(2), 395–408 (1975). APS gives 15 January 1975 as the publication date. Crossref supplies the complete page range.
- **BK55.** The original scan gives the spelling “monotonity,” volume IX (1955), pp. 135–147. The archive catalogue gives 1957 as the physical publication date and a different descriptive page range. The reference follows the printed issue.
- **Dob68.** The Russian journal record gives R. L. Dobrushin and connects the English edition to the cited DOI. The English metadata uses a different spelling and initials.
- **Gri09**, **FH91**, and **Jan97.** The references give the verified original print years. Electronic records may give later dates.
- **Jen12.** The conventional citation is volume 141 (1912), pp. 235–244. Crossref encodes 1912 as the volume and 141 as the issue.
- **MJ00.** The coauthor's institutional publication record and the print book give 2000. Wiley's DOI page gives 3 January 1999. The print reference uses 2000. [St Andrews publication record](https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/directional-statistics/) · [Wiley record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9780470316979)
- **SW64** and **SW71.** The 2000 paperback printing and the 1971 Princeton hardcover, respectively, are documented by the publisher materials. Later copyright or digital publication dates refer to other records or formats.
- **SZZ23** and **vRS05.** The journal issue years 2023 and 2005 are correct. The corresponding articles appeared online in 2022 and 2004. The punctuation of the von Renesse–Sturm title follows the publisher.
