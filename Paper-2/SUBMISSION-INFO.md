# Second-paper details

**Title:** Thermal Purity Bounds for Yang–Mills Theory at Weak Coupling

**Abstract:** We prove sharp thermal-purity bounds, enlarge a one-scale Yang–Mills pressure allowance, and give an explicit finite-volume gap at every finite positive coupling. Exact finite-volume cancellation and error certificates identify the remaining uniform Wilson estimate. That estimate, and the continuum mass gap, remain unproved.

**Keywords:** Yang–Mills theory; thermal purity; spectral gap.

**MSC 2020:** Primary 81T13; Secondary 47B65, 82B20, 81T08.

## Editorial description

This is a separate research follow-up, not a replacement of the published first preprint. It cites DOI 10.20944/preprints202611.1282.v1 and develops sharp thermal-purity certificates, finite-volume all-coupling estimates, finite-order Wilson cancellation, and quantified transport and approximation errors. Its continuum consequence remains conditional on the uniform Wilson pressure estimate. It must not be described in a submission form as an unconditional resolution of the Clay problem.
