# Bibliographic identity checks

Checked for the 15 November 2026 second-paper draft. These are identity and scope checks, not independent validation of every cited proof.

| Reference | Primary source and result |
|---|---|
| First paper | [Preprints.org version 1](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202611.1282/v1): exact title, six authors, date 12 November 2026 and DOI 10.20944/preprints202611.1282.v1 match. The DOI was also checked in the supplied PDF footer. The publisher abstract explicitly leaves the one-scale bound open at weak coupling. |
| Birkhoff, 1957 | [Original AMS article](https://www.ams.org/tran/1957-085-01/S0002-9947-1957-0087058-6/S0002-9947-1957-0087058-6.pdf): author, title, Transactions volume 85 and pages 219–227 match. The manuscript links this original instead of inserting an unneeded identifier. |
| Bushell, 1973 | [Oxford publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/jlms/article-abstract/s2-6/2/256/872989): title, author, volume 6, 256–258, year and DOI 10.1112/jlms/s2-6.2.256 match. |
| Greenberg, 1961 | [Elsevier record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/000349166190032X): title, author, Annals of Physics 16, 158–176 and DOI 10.1016/0003-4916(61)90032-X matched in the primary-record pass. A repeated direct fetch returned 403; it is not a proof that the reference is invalid. The manuscript uses a Wightman generalized-free-field construction, not a divergent unsubtracted Euclidean momentum integral. |
| Jaffe–Witten | [Official Clay statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf): genuine source for the full continuum existence-and-gap problem. It is not evidence that a finite-volume gap solves that problem. |
