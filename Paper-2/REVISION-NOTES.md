# Paper-2 research revision — 15 November 2026

Title: **Thermal Purity Bounds for Yang–Mills Theory at Weak Coupling**.

This is a separate follow-up to the first preprint, DOI 10.20944/preprints202611.1282.v1, which is cited in the manuscript. All six authors are retained. The first paper and its repository files are preserved.

The revision adds the following proved statements:

- The optimal thermal-purity conversion and the enlarged sufficient pressure budget, with exact rational certification.
- An explicit finite-volume Wilson transfer gap at every finite positive coupling. Its constant degenerates in the limits needed for the continuum problem.
- A thermal-trace variance bound, exact Wilson response identity, and the coupling-continuity error budget.
- Finite-order nonwrapping Haar-cumulant cancellation for the actual Wilson partition functions, with a justified Cauchy remainder and a sufficient small-coupling window. This is not an expansion valid at weak coupling.
- Thermal-defect transport and error-stable ascent, with the model-specific inputs kept explicit.
- A finite-rank transfer-operator construction with a sufficient global residual certificate. No cutoff-uniform Wilson residual bound is supplied.

The uniform Wilson partition-function bound along the continuum tuning remains unproved. Consequently the new draft does not claim a solution of the Clay mass-gap problem. The words “at weak coupling” identify the problem studied; they do not assert that its uniform bound has been established.

The PDF is compiled without unresolved references or overfull boxes, and the source ZIP is tested by a separate extraction and compilation. Anaxia/OpenGluon run records and the precise limits of those checks are included. No new DOI or release is asserted for this upload.
