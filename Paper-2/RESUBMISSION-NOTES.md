This version preserves the substantive mathematical content, proofs and appendices of the first version and strengthens its continuum-gap criterion to a single cube at one physical scale. It supplies full derivations of the positive torus comparison, purity conversion, nonlinear ascent, pressure zero–one law and the conditional continuum gap with constant log(63/32)/t0. Exact same-coupling replica and tilted-conditional trace identities identify the remaining Wilson estimates; a gauge-invariant large-field construction rules out a global pointwise contraction shortcut, and spectral-retention and Dirichlet-form arguments separate nontriviality from a lower gap. A volume-independent one-link conditional Wilson inequality, a tilted Fisher-information criterion, an exact bounded-energy retention equivalence and a support-preserving spatial-flow construction further identify the remaining model estimates. The revision includes six new diagrams, an exact-rational appendix, corrected physical-unit conversion, current code-coverage tables and a checked bibliography with the van Baal–Koller author order corrected. All six authors are retained. Data and Code Availability gives the project and Anaxia repository links. The manuscript does not claim to solve the Clay problem: the single-cube inequality along the actual weak-coupling tuning and the required nontrivial continuum construction for every compact simple gauge group remain unproved.

Theorem 20.1 gives a second one-cube criterion. If the Wilson-weighted probability of different seam matchings is at least 2/5 on the cube of side 24T, increasing time eightfold before enlarging space gives a physical gap of at least log(3)/(7t0) in a compatible Wightman limit. The probability lower bound along the weak-coupling tuning and nontrivial continuum existence remain open.
